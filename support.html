I bought a language but it's locked

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases. It's also on the screen where you choose a language. Use the same Apple Account or Google account you bought it with.

Why did my next language cost more?

The first language you buy costs less than the ones after it. Lingavo checks what your Apple Account or Google account has bought, so the price is the same on every phone you use.

I have a code

In Lingavo, go to Settings and tap Redeem a code. You can also redeem it in the App Store or the Play Store, and the language unlocks next time you open Lingavo.

Refunds

  • iPhone: Apple handles refunds. Ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Android: ask Google Play (see Google Play's refund policies), or email us.

Does it need the internet?

No. All the phrases and audio are in the app. You only need a connection to buy a language or restore your purchases.

I can't hear the audio

  • Turn the volume up. Lingavo plays even with the iPhone's silent switch on.
  • If headphones or a Bluetooth speaker are connected, the sound goes there.
  • Play all stops when you lock your phone, unless you turn on Settings → Audio → Keep playing when locked.

Moving to a new phone

Your progress is saved on your phone, not online. Restoring a backup of your old phone onto a new one may bring it across. It can't move between an iPhone and an Android phone. Languages you've bought come back with Restore purchases.

Accessibility

Lingavo is built to work with VoiceOver, TalkBack and large text. If something doesn't work for you, please email us.
