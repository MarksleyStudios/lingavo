Learn what you'll actually use.

Lingavo teaches the phrases you need when you travel: ordering, getting around, asking for help. Learn them on the flight, then use the same app as a phrasebook on the trip.

  • Japanese is free. Other languages are one-off purchases, with no subscription.
  • Every phrase has audio in a female and a male voice, at normal and slow speeds.
  • A guide to saying each phrase, written for English speakers.
  • Flip cards, quizzes and a five-minute review that brings back what you're about to forget.
  • Works completely offline. No account, no ads and no tracking.
