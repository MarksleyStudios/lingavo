Privacy policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

The short version

Lingavo collects no personal data. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you do in the app stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Lingavo saves these on your phone so it can pick up where you left off:

  • your progress, quiz results and review schedule
  • your favourites
  • your settings, such as voice and speed
  • which languages you've bought, so they open straight away

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes your progress and settings. If your phone backs up its apps to iCloud or Google, that backup may include them, under your own account with Apple or Google.

Buying a language

Purchases go through the App Store or Google Play, under Apple's privacy policy or Google's privacy policy. To know which languages to open, the app asks the store which ones your account has bought. That stays between your phone and Apple or Google.

Apple and Google give sellers a record of each sale, such as the product, price and country. We use these only for accounting, tax and refunds. We never see your card or bank details.

When the app uses the internet

Only to buy a language or restore your purchases, through the App Store or Google Play. All the phrases and audio are in the app. Links to the sources a phrase came from open in your web browser. Emergency numbers open your phone app.

Permissions

Lingavo doesn't use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos. If you turn on Keep playing when locked, the phrase being played shows on your lock screen.

If you email us

We use your email address and message only to reply to you, and delete them once they're no longer needed.

Children

Lingavo collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Who we are, and your rights

Lingavo is made by Marksley Studios, an independent developer in the UK. You can ask what we hold about you (at most, emails you've sent us) or ask us to delete it:

marksley.studios@gmail.com

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes

If this ever changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the app changes.
